Snyder ORCID: orcid.org/0000-0002-5565-3063 6 Show authors Nature Neuroscience volume 28 , pages 689–693 ( 2025 ) Cite this article Save article View saved research 16k Accesses 48 Citations 222 Altmetric Metrics details Theories of consciousness have a long and controversial history. One well-known proposal — integrated information theory — has recently been labeled as ‘pseudoscience’, which has caused a heated open debate. Here we discuss the case and argue that the theory is indeed unscientific because its core claims are untestable even in principle.
